Panel and service upgrades in Fergus.

Moving the panel in a Fergus renovation changes where every circuit begins and ends, not only where the box hangs. Where the upper floor has no usable attic, the new location also decides how cable reaches the rooms above.

A panel and an electrical service are different parts of the system

The panel distributes electricity to property circuits. The service is the incoming supply and associated equipment. Replacing a panel does not automatically increase the available supply. A Licensed Electrical Contractor should assess existing condition and demand, planned loads and the utility requirements before recommending equipment. Spare breaker spaces alone do not establish capacity.

Prepare the scope for your Fergus property

Sketch the current panel position and the wall where it might move, and note any stairs, closets or finished ceilings between that wall and the upper floor. Photograph the closed panel door, labels included, from a few steps back.

Ask the contractor how the existing circuits will be extended or rejoined at the new location, which finished surfaces must be opened, and who repairs them. Ask for the answers in the written scope.

What should the assessment establish?

A panel moved during a renovation in Fergus needs a stated reason for the move and a statement of what the new position must keep clear for reach and access. The scope traces how each existing circuit gets to that spot. If a run crosses a finished wall, the crossing is marked, and if none does, the scope says so plainly. Every finished surface to be opened and restored appears with the trade responsible for it, and the record carries the ESA notification and who arranges it. The contractor's findings on the supply and the planned loads are explained as well.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Can the panel move to a more reachable wall during a renovation?

It can, but moving the panel means every circuit must be extended or rejoined, so the route matters as much as the destination. Ask the contractor to show the proposed position and the path to the upper floor.

Why does the space around a relocated panel matter for access?

A panel needs clear working space in front of it so it can be reached and serviced safely. Ask the contractor to confirm in writing that the new position keeps that space clear, and to note anything stored nearby that would need to move.
